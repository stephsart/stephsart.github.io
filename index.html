About the Artist

Stephanie Kleine is a self-taught painter living in the greater Seattle area. She has been painting since her teenage years, and it always starts the same way: with a feeling she can't shake, or something that happened and won't leave her alone. There is no plan. No sketch. She grabs a brush, puts paint on a canvas and sees where it takes her.

She paints in two styles. One is loud: abstract and colorful, full of shapes and movement. Jackson Pollock and Saburo Murakami are two of her biggest idols. The other is quiet. A black background and a single human organ, drawn in fine lines of one bright color. Anatomy fascinates her. How a living body actually works. How much power a single organ holds over everything else.

She never knows when a painting is finished. It tortures her. Once the emotion is worked through, the painting still has to become something she enjoys looking at, and that is when her perfectionism shows up. She will work on the smallest detail for months without really changing anything. That is when she knows it is time to put the brush down.

Lungs filled with Blue Smoke is one of her most honest paintings. She used to be a heavy smoker. Then she quit. This painting is that journey.
