The Artist

About

I have been painting since I was 14. Growing up in Germany, I longed for more: experiencing different cultures, breaking out of societal norms and finding my Why. I moved to the Netherlands and Ireland before finally settling down in the greater Seattle area.

I paint in two styles. One is abstract and colorful, full of shapes and movement, free of any rules but the ones I set myself. Jackson Pollock and Saburo Murakami are two of my biggest creative idols. The other is focused: one object, fine lines, often dark colors clashing with bright ones.

Painting has always been a therapeutic medium for me. Healing Lungs, a painting from my Anatomy Done Differently series, tells the story of quitting cigarettes after eight years of smoking.

I’m continuing the series and have also started experimenting with digital art centered around emotions, still drawing from my curiosity about human anatomy. Stay tuned.
